# Le Council du Vin · what has changed

A running record for the members: every release, and the unseen work behind it. Newest first.

## 10 August 2026

- **The vine holds its tongue in earnest.** The Prophecy was only ever meant to speak once every soul who has answered the call has sealed an offering, but that condition was tested only before its first word: once the vine had spoken, the door stood open ever after. Now the invitation itself stays dark until the room is complete, and says why. A verdict already given still reveals, as it must, for the vine cannot un-know what it knew.
- **A door that would not open.** One member had been shut out since the day he was anointed, told at every attempt that he was known to the gate but not yet of it. The cause was a single capital letter in the address on his record. The door now reads an address as any post office would, caring nothing for capitals or a stray space, and the same blindness was given to every check that decides rank and standing.

## 18 July 2026

- **A door that would not open.** One member had been shut out of the Council since the day he was anointed, told at every attempt that he was known to the gate but not yet of it. The cause was a single capital letter in the address on his record, which the instrument compared too strictly to recognise as his own. The door now reads an address as any post office would, caring nothing for capitals or a stray space, and the same blindness has been given to every check that decides rank and standing, so no soul can be turned away by their own spelling again.

- **Answer a call, and the night finds your calendar.** Say you are coming on the convening and a letter follows at once, carrying the gathering itself. Open the attachment and the night takes its place in your calendar, theme, hour, host and door code with it, and your own device will stir the evening before so no bottle is chosen in haste. The letter tells you plainly what to tap, whichever way you read your mail. Withdraw your answer and nothing is sent: the night is yours to remove.
- **Behind the curtain: built for the ways these usually fail.** A calendar invitation is a fussy thing, and most arrive as a dead file, land an hour out, or breed duplicates every time they are sent. The Council's own is written to the letter of the standard, and stands behind thirty-eight automated checks covering exactly those three failures. Send it twice and it amends the night rather than doubling it. The Keiser can prove the whole path on his own vessel before a single member receives one.

## 17 July 2026

- **Sleep, made real.** A sleeping seat could still type into its own record: the fields were live even though the Council would not have kept a word of it. Now every field on every page falls dead to a sleeping hand, not merely the buttons. One thing stays open, by the Keiser's decree: **photographs of past nights**. A sleeping soul may still hang a picture in the codex, though never touch their own portrait.
- **The Keiser may take a photograph down.** Any picture in the codex, gone from the night and from the vault both, after a confirm. Nobody else may.
- **The chalice champion leaves the codex head.** The victories card below already tells that story.

- **A seat may now sleep.** The Keiser can put any seat to sleep, and wake it again at a word. A sleeping member is not cast out and loses nothing: the Council remembers them, every chamber their rank opens stays open to their eyes, and their whole record stands untouched. But a sleeping hand writes nothing: no ballot, no offering, no answer to a call, no counsel, not a word of their own record. They are told so plainly on every page, the roll shows their seat greyed and sleeping for all to see, and the controls they cannot use fall quiet rather than pretending. Their own sky remains theirs to read and to send to themselves: sleep takes the pen, not the stars.
- **The night can no longer be lost.** A soul put to sleep after answering a call used to be waited on forever: the cloths could never lift and the night could never reach the Annals. The rite now never waits on a sleeping hand, and the vine no longer holds its tongue for an offering that can never come.
- **Behind the curtain: the seal was audited before it was believed.** Fifty independent readings swept every write path in the instrument and found sixteen ways a sleeping hand could still write, most through doors the first attempt had not even closed. The seal was rebuilt at the one gate every write must pass, then audited again by fresh eyes, which caught a fault in the repair itself: it had welded a sleeping soul's answer in place so that not even the Keiser could withdraw it. Both passes are recorded, and the rules now stand behind 47 automated checks of their own.
- **A word from the sleeping.** A sleeping seat may send exactly one thing: a plea to wake, carried to the Keiser by hand. It writes nothing to the Council's records; it only asks.
- **Tarnished chalices.** While a seat sleeps, its victories show dulled on the card. Nothing is ever taken away: every crown still stands in the annals, and the gold brightens the moment the seat wakes.
- **Any night, posted to you.** Every gathering in the codex now carries "Email this to me": the full Reckoning of that night, the crowning, the ranked table, the split cloth, the ledger, sent months or years later exactly as it read on the night. Until now the Reckoning was reachable only until the next gathering was summoned.
- **The Reckoning email, reworked.** The title is simply the gathering, its numeral now legible rather than lost in script; the crowned wine stands on its own line with the hand that bore it beneath; and the ledger names that hand too, not just the bottle.
- **Behind the curtain: the vault, not just the door.** The instrument's lock is a courtesy; anyone determined can speak to the vault directly. Every record of the Council, and three old doors found standing unguarded since the first days, are now sealed in the database itself.
- **Behind the curtain: a fourth door, and a locksmith's audit.** Sealing those three doors revealed a fourth: an old view that read past every lock by design, quietly exposing what the locks were meant to guard. It is gone. The live vault was then checked table by table, column by column, against the Council's own plans: no drift, nothing adrift.
- **Behind the curtain: the Council's voice, corrected.** Every page and every email was swept for a punctuation the Keiser has banished; thirty-two survivors were put right in his own voice. The sweep uncovered a fault it would otherwise have hidden: the vine's refusals had begun to announce themselves twice.

## 15 July 2026

Polish and truth-telling, in one breath:

- **The crown, corrected.** On a night recorded without scores, the wine listed first now takes the crown, exactly as the record reads. Pour numbers no longer decide standings anywhere.
- **The Augury, steadied.** Consulting the Augury now lands you at the top of the card, and the stray close mark that haunted its corner on phones is banished. Past petitioners each appear once, wearing their truest record.
- **Your profile, tidier.** Your sky now sits directly beneath your own details. The roster and your venue instructions can be folded away (they arrive open), and the feature request waits folded until called.
- **The Reckoning's envelope, always visible.** The email-this-to-me option at the foot of every Reckoning now shows itself in every mode.
- **Behind the curtain: the verification charts labelled.** The fixed test charts that guard the astrology engines are now marked as fixtures in the code, so no test sky can ever be mistaken for a member's true reading. The crowning rules gained fresh automated checks written from this week's findings before the fix shipped.

## 11 July 2026

The Council's instrument has been rebuilt in near silence, and the work is now yours. One list, everything new:

- **The Heavens.** Every member card now turns over. One gold door opens three chambers: the Wheel (your natal sky), the Foretelling, and the Kundli. Choose the day, the moon, or the year, through a Western or a Vedic lens, and any view can be sent to your own inbox with one press.
- **The Kundli.** A full Vedic birth reading: your chart drawn in both the North and South Indian manner, your kindred stars (how your moon accords with every member of the table), your auspicious hours, your yogas, and the pillars and pitfalls of the chart, every passage written by hand in the Council's voice.
- **The Foretelling.** Real transits against your own chart, day by day, month by month, and the year's turnings, including the iron passage of Saturn with exact dates. Nothing is padded: a quiet month reads quiet.
- **The Tribunal opens.** Members may now read every petition at the gate and cast counsel: anoint, cast out, or abstain. Once the Keiser decrees, how the Council counselled is recorded forever. Petitioners now show their portrait, and date, time, and place of birth are required of all who knock.
- **The Augury.** For any petitioner with a complete birth record, the Council may consult the sky before judging: their accord with the whole table, whom they will sit easiest and hardest beside, the shape of their palate, and one honest warning. The card turns over to reveal it.
- **The Prophecy.** Before each gathering the vine weighs everything it knows, each bringer's record, the room's grape leanings, the room's weakness for price, and names the soul it expects to be crowned. Its record is graded in the codex under "prophecies fulfilled".
- **The Reckoning.** When a night is committed, the reveal becomes a full account: the crowning, the ranked table, the split cloth that divided the room, the table's anonymous whispers, the value ledger, the prophecy's grade, the photographs, and an email of the whole record to yourself.
- **The Reliquary grows.** All-time records now include the highest pour ever, the great schism, the iron palate and the gentle hand, the crowned grape, and the price of victory.
- **The codex, enriched.** New measures across the top: coin poured in rand, prophecies fulfilled, and a Grapes Tasted bar counting the distinct varietals the table has judged against the full ledger of 177 grapes.
- **The curse of the first cloth.** A chart of the average verdict by pouring order, counted only where the pour was truly recorded. The findings may surprise you: the first pour rules, and the seventh is where wines go to die.
- **Sixteen nights of history.** The Council's full record from before the instrument existed has been imported: real ballots, real notes, real crowns, all of it now feeding the dossiers and the records.
- **Your dossier, deeper.** Member cards carry the Palate Dossier: moons stood, bottles crowned, marks against, palate temper, value for coin, the purse, kindred palate, the wheel, longest communion, your Nose (the aromas you mark most), and your three finest pours. The chalice count follows, then the birth-derived signs, then the door to the Heavens.
- **Grapes and coin everywhere.** Every offering and every codex wine can carry its varietals (auto-detected from the name) and its price, which is what feeds the value ledger, the prophecy, and the new records.
- **The crown, made true.** A disqualified first place is never credited anywhere. On a night recorded without scores, the listed order carries the standing. Pour numbers are now editable in the codex and may be left blank where the pouring order was never known, so no false history is ever recorded.
- **A cleaner face.** The tab row is gone: the chambers now fold behind a hamburger in the top bar, with a quiet wine-red dot when petitions await. Number fields lose their fiddly arrows, tooltips behave on a phone (one at a time, always on screen), and the pyramid mark stands in for every unset portrait.
- **Behind the curtain: every formula quadruple-checked.** Seven verification suites, more than 150 automated checks, now stand guard over the mathematics: the Vedic engine, the Kundli's tables, the transits and dashas, the crowning rules, the dossier counts, and the Augury. They run before anything ships.
- **Behind the curtain: independent audits.** Every sourced table in the astrology, the kuta tables, the dasha years, the ayanamsa, the nakshatra boundaries, the panchang methods, was audited by separate research passes against the classical sources before being trusted.
- **Behind the curtain: no oracle but our own.** The instrument has been decoupled from every external astrology service and library. No API of any kind is consulted: every chart, transit, dasha, and kuta is computed in-house by the Council's own verified engines. That is the whole doctrine.

The vine calculates. It does not flatter.
