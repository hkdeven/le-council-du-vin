one door; the envelope lives inside, at the foot of every view, and sends the view you are reading

The rule this establishes: charts are stops, time is one stop with three horizons, and every stop ends with its own envelope. Anything we add later (varshaphala, honours, the tome’s sky pages) gets a stop or a horizon, never a new button. Voice still flips on another’s chart; initiates still see only what their rank allows; the veiled gate guards all three stops at once.
