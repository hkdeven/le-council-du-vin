What has changed
  • The victor wears a faceThe member who brought the winning bottle now appears beside it. Nobody else does.
  • Every verdict, openableTap any bottle to see what the whole table gave it and what each of us wrote. Your own verdict sits at the top.
  • The pour number survivesThe circled number is the order it was poured, and it stays beside the wine even once the bottle is named. The place it took stays in roman numerals, so the two can never be confused.
  • One wine, brought twiceIdentical bottles are bound into a single entry with one score, the average of them all, and a single place. Each pour number is kept.
  • A spoiled bottleMarked on the night, recorded forever, and counted against no one: not the ranking, not the bearer’s average, not the purse.
  • GoneThe table’s whispers. Every note now lives on the bottle it was written about.
