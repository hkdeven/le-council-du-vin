The two warnings

Neither stops a member sealing the wine. One stops the page.

1 · Someone else logged this wine tonight

Someone else has already logged this wine for the night!

Bring it if you must, but your score and ranking will be pooled and averaged with the other matching bottles. You are highly encouraged to bring a different bottle.

Understood

A popup over the whole page, dismissed by the button, by Escape, or by tapping outside it. It never names who logged the other bottle.
