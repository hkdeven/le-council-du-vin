whole-sign houses · computed from the true sky

Nothing here is guessed or generated. Your birth moment is first converted from local time to Universal Time using the historical timezone database, so old dates carry the offsets of their era. Each position is then computed from established astronomical formulae: the sun from its true ecliptic longitude, the moon from a perturbed lunar ephemeris, the planets from their orbital elements, and the ascendant from the exact sidereal time at your birthplace's latitude and longitude. Houses follow the whole-sign tradition.

The engine is tested against documented reference charts and known sky events: cusp-day birthdays, recorded full and new moons, and historical charts with published ascendants. Where a detail is missing, a birth time or place, the chart shows nothing rather than an estimate.

the vine calculates, it does not flatter

The Foretelling is computed, never invented. The positions of the planets for the month and year ahead are calculated by the same astronomical engine as your natal chart, then compared against your own chart: a transit is reported only when a real geometric alignment occurs, with its true dates. Moon phases and retrogrades are astronomy, exact to the day.

The words that interpret each alignment are written once, by hand, in the Council's voice, and chosen by the alignment itself, never at random. Two members under the same transit will read the same truth differently placed, because the houses it lands in are theirs alone. No machine writes these words, and nothing is padded to fill a quiet month: a quiet month reads quiet.
