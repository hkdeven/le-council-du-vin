The figures

Sun conjunct Pluto, exact: a third of a degree apart in Scorpio’s 2nd house. Identity and power share one seat; you do not want things mildly, and what you value, you guard like a vault.

The keeper of your chart stands in her own sign at the very door of it: dignified, at home, and visible. Charm is not something you do; it is the material the whole chart is built from, and it strengthens every other placement it touches.

The birth moon
The last quarter

the waning half-light · Sun and Moon 309° apart

Born as the moon turned away from fullness: the crisis of conscience. Last-quarter souls harvest and prune; they take what a cycle has taught, keep the essential, and clear ground for what others cannot yet see coming.

All four readings are computed, never guessed: elements and modes counted from the placements, figures detected by aspect geometry, the bearer from the rising sign’s ruler and its dignity, the birth moon from the Sun-Moon angle.
